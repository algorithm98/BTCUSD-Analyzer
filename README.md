📈 BTCUSD Analyzer

🚀 AI-powered Bitcoin market analysis and trading intelligence platform.

⸻

✨ Product Snapshot

BTCUSD Analyzer is an advanced cryptocurrency analysis platform focused on delivering real-time Bitcoin market insights, technical analysis, and AI-driven trading intelligence.

Built for traders, analysts, and developers, the platform combines market data, technical indicators, and intelligent analytics to help users better understand BTC/USD market movements.

⸻

🎯 The Problem

Crypto markets are:

* ⚡ Extremely volatile
* 📊 Data-heavy and difficult to interpret
* ⏱️ Fast-moving with real-time decision pressure

Traditional dashboards only show charts — they don’t help users understand the market context.

⸻

💡 The Solution

BTCUSD Analyzer solves this by:

* 📈 Providing real-time BTC/USD analysis
* 🧠 Generating intelligent market insights
* ⚡ Simplifying technical analysis workflows
* 🤖 Creating a foundation for AI-assisted trading systems

⸻

1. 📡 Fetches BTC/USD market data
